How to Play Blockr

Blockr is a free daily puzzle. Fill every open square on the grid with the coloured pieces and try to complete the challenge as quickly as possible.

Place the pieces

Drag each coloured piece from the tray onto the grid. A piece must fit entirely within the open squares and cannot overlap a blocked square or another piece.

Rotate a piece

Click or tap a piece once to rotate it. Keep clicking or tapping to cycle through its available positions, then drag it onto the grid.

Change your placement

You can move a placed piece to another open position. You can also drag it back to the tray to remove it and try a different arrangement.

Use a hint

If you get stuck, use the Hint button for help. Hints used are recorded with your result, so try to solve the puzzle without one when you can.

Complete the daily challenge

The puzzle is solved when every open square is filled correctly. Your completion time, hints used and solve streak appear in Today’s Result.

Share and challenge

After completing the puzzle, use Share Today’s Results to share your achievement, or Challenge a Friend and see whether they can beat your time.
